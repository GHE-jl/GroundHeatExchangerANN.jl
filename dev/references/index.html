References

The networks implemented in this package are drawn from the following sources.

  • Pasquier, P., Zarrella, A., & Labib, R. (2018). Application of artificial neural networks to near-instant construction of short-term g-functions. Applied Thermal Engineering, 143, 910–921. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.applthermaleng.2018.07.137 The PublishedANN network (85 nodes, 7-day horizon).

  • Pasquier, P., & Marcotte, D. (2020). Robust identification of volumetric heat capacity and analysis of thermal response tests by Bayesian inference with correlated residuals. Applied Energy, 261, 114394. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2019.114394 The DeepANN network (95 nodes, 21-day horizon, wider validity ranges).
